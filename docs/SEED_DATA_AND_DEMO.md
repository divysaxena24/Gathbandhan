# Seed Data and Demo Scenario

## Anchor wedding
- Couple: Aarav and Meera Sharma.
- Wedding title: Sharma Royal Wedding.
- Destination: Udaipur, Rajasthan.
- Package: Full-Service Wedding.
- Duration: 3 days.
- Budget: ₹2 crore.
- Guest estimate: 400; comparison scenario 480.
- Events: Mehendi, Sangeet, Wedding Ceremony, Reception.
- Vendors: approximately 18.
- Tasks: 30+ with owners, dates and dependencies.
- Client payment schedule: booking, advance around 30 days before, final settlement after event.
- Three caterer quotes with intentionally different GST/service/travel/overtime/inclusion terms.
- At least one vendor advance, one invoice variance and approved extra guests.
- Event-day DJ late scenario and an incident with resolution.
- Keep all financial totals mathematically consistent.

## Season
Seed about 25 weddings with:
- Upcoming, in-progress and completed statuses.
- Varied budgets and packages.
- Diverse projected/final margins.
- Some outstanding client receivables.
- Some vendor payables due soon.
- Vendor punctuality and incident history.
- Team workload variation.
- Do not make all records unrealistically perfect.

## Demo story
1. Create/inspect lead.
2. Compare caterers at 400 and 480 guests.
3. Select vendor and generate wedding plan.
4. Simulate DJ late check-in and cascade impact.
5. Reconcile extra guest invoice.
6. Try duplicate payment; show safe prevention.
7. Open founder season dashboard and show profit, cash due and vendor reliability.

All seeded content must be clearly marked as demo data.
