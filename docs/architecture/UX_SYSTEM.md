# UX System and Design Guidelines

## Product character
Premium, calm, capable, operational. Luxury wedding agency aesthetic without sacrificing legibility or speed.

## Visual direction
- Warm ivory or off-white application surfaces.
- Charcoal navigation and typography.
- Restrained muted gold for brand accents only.
- Neutral grays for ordinary states.
- Semantic green/amber/red for success/warning/critical.
- Elegant display font may be used for brand/large titles; use a highly legible sans-serif for tables and operational data.
- Avoid heavy gradients, excessive glassmorphism and dashboard-card clutter.

## UX principles
- Surface next action and risks before decorative analytics.
- Every data table supports search/filter/sort where useful.
- Use clear INR formatting and indicate assumptions.
- Distinguish projected, committed, invoiced, paid and outstanding values.
- Make event-day controls large, touch-friendly and usable outdoors on mobile.
- QR check-in should require minimal interaction and no account creation for the vendor if using a secure one-time link.
- Provide empty/loading/error/success states.
- Avoid false precision; display source/last-updated information for computed metrics.
- Confirmation dialogs for destructive or financial actions.
- Keep client portal visually simpler than internal agency workspace.
