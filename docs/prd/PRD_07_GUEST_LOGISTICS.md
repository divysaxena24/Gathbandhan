# PRD 07 — Guest RSVP & Logistics

## Goal
Track guest and household attendance per event and coordinate dietary needs, accommodation and travel.

## Requirements
- Guest and household/group records.
- Secure public RSVP link scoped to wedding/guest/group.
- RSVP for each wedding event, not one wedding-wide response.
- Plus-one count, meal selection, dietary requirements/allergies, accessibility notes with privacy care.
- Attendance estimates by event: Mehendi, Sangeet, ceremony, reception.
- Expected vs confirmed vs actual headcount.
- Configurable catering buffer and walk-in allowance.
- Dietary summary for caterer, restricted to necessary details.
- Accommodation room allocation, check-in/out, hotel and confirmation.
- Transport/pickup assignment with time, location, vehicle and status.
- Export guest list with access controls.

## Privacy
Guest personal data and allergy information are sensitive operational data. Limit access to assigned wedding team and appropriate vendors. Do not expose full guest details to unrelated vendors.

## Acceptance criteria
- RSVP link updates correct event-specific attendance.
- Event totals update correctly.
- Dietary summary can be generated for authorized caterer.
- Room and pickup assignments are visible to authorized coordinators.
- Unauthorized user cannot enumerate guests.
