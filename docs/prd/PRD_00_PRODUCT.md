# PRD 00 — Product Overview

## Product
The Royal Knot OS — Wedding Operations & Financial Management Platform.

## Problem
The Royal Knot manages approximately 25 luxury weddings per season, each with 400+ guests, around 18 vendors and multi-crore budgets. Enquiries, vendor quotes, tasks, payments and profit data are fragmented across WhatsApp, PDFs, diaries and spreadsheets. This creates forgotten leads, manual quote comparisons, missed advances, event-day confusion and delayed profitability insight.

## Product goals
1. Centralize the full wedding lifecycle.
2. Reduce coordination risk and missed commitments.
3. Compare vendor offers using normalized landed cost and service fit.
4. Generate actionable wedding plans from templates and dates.
5. Coordinate event day with live check-ins and incident handling.
6. Prevent duplicate/incorrect payments and reconcile extras.
7. Provide trustworthy projected and final wedding/season profitability.

## Packages
- Full-Service: theme/design, venue, vendor sourcing, layout, guest tracking, rehearsal, event-day management.
- Partial / Day-of: schedule management, vendor check-in, family movement coordination, event closeout.

## Personas
Founder/Admin, Planner, Finance Manager, On-site Coordinator, Vendor, Client/Couple.

## Non-goals for initial MVP
- Replacing a full accounting/ERP system.
- Automated bank payment execution.
- AI autonomous approval of financial changes.
- Full offline editing of every domain.
- Full native mobile apps.
- Guaranteed legally qualified digital signature without provider integration.

## Success metrics
- No lead without an owner/follow-up date.
- All wedding tasks have owner and due date or documented exception.
- Vendor quote comparison shows cost assumptions and inclusion gaps.
- Event-day late vendor surfaced within configured grace interval.
- Payment transaction retry does not duplicate ledger entry.
- Founder sees projected and finalized profit with definitions and as-of time.
- Core workflows pass E2E tests.

## Global acceptance
- One record of truth per wedding.
- Server-side role checks.
- Auditability for financial and contractual changes.
- INR formatting and Asia/Kolkata display.
- Responsive interface and realistic seeded data.
